PRIME X: The Future

Prime X is a production management website designed to digitally track daily production activities in factories and assembly lines.

Users log in based on their role—Data Incharge, Line Leader, or Manager. Line Leaders and Data Incharges enter real-time production data such as input used, packed output, rejection, and rework details.

This data is automatically organized and shown on a central dashboard, giving managers a clear overview of production performance, efficiency, and losses.

Prime X reduces manual work, minimizes errors, and helps management make faster and more accurate decisions.

This allows them to spend more time on actual production instead of paperwork. For Sohail, this project is an important step and the beginning of many practical solutions he plans to build in the future."
