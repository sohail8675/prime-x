PRIME X: The Future

Traditional manufacturing relies on assumptions. Prime X relies on Precision. We replaced manual registers with a unified Digital Backbone.

Solved: The "Double Counting" Problem

In multi-line assembly, moving parts from Line 1 to Line 3 often results in double counting. Prime X uses Component-Level Tracking to distinguish between 'Parts Made' and 'Final Output'.

  • Live Telemetry: Data moves from floor to dashboard in milliseconds.
  • Digital Handover: Shift transfers happen digitally.
  • Quality Grading: Automatic A/B/C grading of lines based on rejection.
